OUR HERITAGE

Tradition Meets Culinary Passion

Situated on Taha Hussein Street right by the Military Accounts Complex in Benghazi, 508 Pizza was born out of a relentless dedication to authentic Italian pizza craftsmanship.

We believe great pizza begins with uncompromising quality: flour imported for elasticity and crunch, tomatoes ripened under the Mediterranean sun, and time-honored baking techniques.
