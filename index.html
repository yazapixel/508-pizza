OUR STORY

Passion for Perfect Pizza

Located on Taha Hussein Street in Benghazi, 508 Pizza was founded with a singular mission: to bring exceptional artisan pizza crafted with dedication, premium ingredients, and authentic recipes to our community.

Every single pizza is hand-stretched, layered with our signature house-made tomato sauce, topped with 100% real mozzarella, and baked until bubbling and golden.
